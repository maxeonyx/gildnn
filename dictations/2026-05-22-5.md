We just strengthened the background run process. The specific change: when delegating experiment work that may involve a long GPU run, the delegation must be split into two separate phases. Phase one is launch only — the subagent sets up and starts the experiment, returns the PID and log path, and stops. It does not wait, does not poll, does not check results. Phase two is check and analyse — the orchestrator resumes the subagent later, after doing other useful work in between. The orchestrator is responsible for deciding when to check back, not the subagent. If the orchestrator delegates without splitting it this way, it has failed the process.
