I've got another idea I'd like to try. Essentially, I would like to see if it's possible to make a network that not only predicts its inputs, but that tries to predict its own outputs. And own internal latents. That way can we compress computation naturally into fewer time steps. Rather than having a network that is forced somehow to learn to process one token given its size, have a network that learns to process many or much ahead with smaller size. Perhaps this is the same as producing multiple tokens at once. Or perhaps it's the same as the dynamic depth. I'm not quite sure. But to me it seems an interesting idea to train a network not just to predict output from input, but to train it to do so "quicker" somehow.
