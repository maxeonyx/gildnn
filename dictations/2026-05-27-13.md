# Dictation 2026-05-27-13

Redaction: dictation 2026-05-27-12 was edited. The support session added incorrect analysis claiming multi-rate = halting/depth and "blocks run when surprised, not on a fixed schedule." That conflates two separate mechanisms:

- **Multi-rate** = how often each block fires across timesteps (higher blocks fire less often, bridging longer temporal extent)
- **Depth/halting** = how many recurrent iterations within a single timestep

These are independent dimensions. The accumulation-based rate thresholds are about multi-rate triggering, not about halting.

Additionally: the model's goal is building high-level representations, not being good at predicting tokens.
