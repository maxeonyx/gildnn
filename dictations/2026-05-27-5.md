# Dictation 2026-05-27-5

## Building up is fine, but not by training broken models

It's okay to build up to the target model incrementally. But "build up" means testing the PIECES individually — not training smaller models that are broken versions of the full thing.

Building up by testing a flat GRU, then a multi-block GRU with global CE, then adding local loss later — that's wrong. Each intermediate model is broken in a way that makes its results meaningless for my design.

Building up by: "does a single block with tied readout and local CE learn to predict 1 step ahead? (30 seconds) Yes. Does a second block with local CE learn to predict 4 steps ahead? (30 seconds) Yes. Do they produce different representations? Good. Now wire them together with stale laterals. Does it still work? Good. Add noise. Still works? Good. Now train for real." — that's right. Each step tests a piece that's faithful to the final design, not a distortion of it.
