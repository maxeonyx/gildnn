# Dictation 2026-05-27-1

## All experiments use wrong sequence length

There's something deeply wrong with our tests — they're all using tiny context length. SHORT_CONTEXT = 4 everywhere! Why would we do this? I guess because the transformer baselines would be hard to train on a comparable baseline?

I was expecting we'd be doing many thousands of tokens. We have an RNN! We don't care about context length — only batch size and weight size matter for per-step cost.

We're also supposed to do limited BPTT (yet still learn from long sequences somehow), so I don't see why we shouldn't try long sequence lengths. I don't mean long training necessarily — but without long sequences we can't see anything from our architecture.

The correct comparison is: transformer at its sweet spot (attention over 128-256 tokens) vs our RNN at its sweet spot (thousands of tokens, truncated BPTT), at matched parameter count. We've been hobbling the RNN to match the transformer's interface.

The previous negative results about recurrence and stale laterals — those were all tested with 4-char windows. They might be completely wrong once you give the architecture sequences long enough for the hidden state to actually accumulate useful temporal structure.
