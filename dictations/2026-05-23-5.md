## Predictive processing / hierarchical prediction

I'm thinking sometimes about this: if we've got activations that flow away from the inputs and activations that flow back towards the inputs. The ones flowing away are like processed representations of the input and they are like bottom-up information and the ones flowing back down are like priors and top-down information for how to interpret the input. How can we operationalize predictive processing? How can we ensure that whatever architecture we choose, the blocks sort of naturally push towards better and better prediction of the future. They don't get fixed at a certain level of abstraction. They continue to make something more and more abstract as long as it can explain more and more of the data. Instead of predicting the raw output, which is noisy, can a higher level module predict the higher level features that explain the low level features — the latents that explain the low level features? That way, block one tries to predict the next word, but block two tries to predict the features that predict the distribution over the next word, which are a lot more stable and high level.
